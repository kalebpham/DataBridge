# DataBridge

**Connecting classroom learning with real-world data projects.**

DataBridge is a proposed student-led club at the **University of Alberta** for students interested in statistics, data science, and programming. We plan to work on independent research and projects with community partners.

The club is in development and has not yet received official student group recognition.

**Co-Presidents:** Estella Li and Kaleb Pham.

## Pilot Project 1: Can We Forecast Edmonton's Worst Pothole Weeks?

**Research question:** Can recent weather conditions improve forecasts of Edmonton's weekly pothole-related 311 reports compared with historical seasonal patterns?

**Objective:** Test whether adding temperature, freeze–thaw cycles, and precipitation improves weekly forecast accuracy over a simple seasonal baseline.

We will combine [Edmonton's 311 data](https://data.edmonton.ca/d/q7ua-agfg) with [historical weather observations](https://climate.weather.gc.ca/). Forecasts will use only information available before the week being predicted and will be evaluated on later weeks.

**Status:** Planning. Data coverage and the study period still need confirmation. No research findings are available yet.

### Final Deliverables

- **Main deliverable:** one short research report, written in **LaTeX** and exported as a **PDF**. Key figures and forecast comparisons will go inside the report.
- **Optional ways to share the findings:** a club website post, a research poster, or a simple dashboard. We will choose what is useful; we do not need to produce all three.

**What “worst weeks” means:** weeks with unusually high numbers of pothole reports. Reports reflect what people submit to 311, not every physical pothole.

## Research Process

Our workflow follows SCI 201's scientific process and adds a final project review step.

### 1. Research Planning

- Identify the research problem, questions, and objectives.
- Review background information and existing literature.
- Define the methodology, data sources, and project scope.

### 2. Data Collection & Preparation

- Collect data from relevant sources and APIs.
- Clean, validate, and combine datasets.
- Develop reproducible data-processing steps.

### 3. Data Analysis & Modelling

- Conduct exploratory data analysis (EDA).
- Apply suitable statistical methods.
- Develop and evaluate predictive models where applicable.

### 4. Research Reporting & Visualization

- Create clear visualizations and optional interactive dashboards.
- Prepare research reports explaining findings, conclusions, and limitations.
- Present and publish reviewed research outcomes.

### 5. Project Review & Completion

- Review research quality and validate findings.
- Finalize and document the agreed deliverables.
- Evaluate the process and record improvements for future projects.

The first four phases draw on the supplied SCI 201 Fall 2026 syllabus, pp. 1 and 5. The fifth phase is DataBridge's additional project review step. DataBridge is an independent club project.
