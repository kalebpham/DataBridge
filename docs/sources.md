# Sources

This is a short reading list for the project. Summaries were prepared with AI assistance and need team review. Sources were checked on November 11, 2026.

## Data and local context

- [City of Edmonton — 311 Requests](https://data.edmonton.ca/d/q7ua-agfg): the proposed data source. Initial checks confirm the pothole category includes both reports and information inquiries; the final filter needs review.
- [City of Edmonton — 311 Open Data guide](https://www.edmonton.ca/programs_services/311-open-data): explains the data and its geographic limits. Published locations can represent neighbourhood centres rather than exact potholes.
- [City of Edmonton — Repair application](https://transforming.edmonton.ca/new-tool-in-battle-against-edmontons-potholes/) (May 21, 2025): describes existing City work on pothole inspections, reporting, and repairs.

## Three useful research papers

| Paper | Why it matters |
| --- | --- |
| Lu, Q., & Johnson, P. A. (2016). *Characterizing New Channels of Communication: A Case Study of Municipal 311 Requests in Edmonton, Canada*. Urban Planning, 1(2), 18–31. [Read](https://doi.org/10.17645/up.v1i2.621). | Shows that Edmonton reporting patterns vary across communication channels. |
| Cook, S. J., Zuhlke, S., & Saywitz, R. (2024). *Potholes, 311 reports, and a theory of heterogeneous resident demand for city services*. Policy Studies Journal, 52(3), 647–669. [Read](https://doi.org/10.1111/psj.12540). | Distinguishes resident reports from underlying pothole service needs in Houston. |
| White, A., & Trump, K.-S. (2018). *The Promises and Pitfalls of 311 Data*. Urban Affairs Review, 54(4), 794–823. [Read](https://doi.org/10.1177/1078087416673202). | Explains why 311 counts need careful interpretation and are not a general measure of civic participation. |

**What this means for us:** describe reporting patterns clearly, explain missing information and reporting bias, and avoid treating report counts as a complete inventory of road damage.

## Research process and responsible data use

- **SCI 201 — Scientific Process, Fall 2026 syllabus, pp. 1 and 5:** the supplied source for the four research phases. Course materials are not redistributed here.
- [Open Government Licence — Edmonton](https://data.edmonton.ca/stories/s/Open-Government-Licence-Edmonton/msh8-if28/): check whether it applies to the selected dataset and follow its attribution requirements. The general licence text was verified; dataset-specific checks remain open.
- [University of Alberta — Do I need research ethics approval?](https://www.ualberta.ca/en/research/services/research-ethics/do-i-need-research-ethics-approval.html): use this guidance if uncertain about the planned data use. Public access alone does not settle every privacy or ethics question.
